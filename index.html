Local Knowledge

Why Pahrump?

About an hour to Las Vegas

Pahrump offers desert space, small-town community and big-city access, about an hour from Las Vegas.

No state income tax

Nevada doesn’t tax personal income at the state level. Check with a tax professional on how it applies to you.

Room for every budget

Condos to mansions, fixer-uppers to luxury, plus investment and commercial properties, with mountain views in every direction.

Adventure nearby

The Spring Mountains and Death Valley National Park are within a day trip, with local wineries and golf close to home.
